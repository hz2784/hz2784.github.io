Frequently asked

What's a typical engagement like?

It really depends! Sometimes it's a one-off workshop for a bookstore or school. Other times it's more of an ongoing thing — designing a reading program, building a recommendation tool, consulting on accessibility. We'd just figure out what makes sense for your situation.

How long does a project take?

A workshop or event design is usually 1–2 weeks. Product stuff like app design or reading assessments tends to be more like 4–8 weeks. For bigger research projects, we'd scope it out together.

Do you work remotely?

Yep! I work remotely and I'm happy to hop on a call whenever works for you. For in-person workshops or events, we can figure out logistics together.

What exactly is bibliotherapy?

Not self-help books. Not reading-as-productivity. It's the kind of reading where something loosens — you close the book and you're not quite the same person who opened it, though you can't say exactly why. I ground my practice in Jung, Hillman, and Landy.

Can you help with dyslexia-specific design?

Yes, absolutely — it's a huge part of what I do. I design reading experiences, interfaces, and programs for people with dyslexia and ADHD, drawing on research around flow states and long-form attention. Happy to chat about what you have in mind.
